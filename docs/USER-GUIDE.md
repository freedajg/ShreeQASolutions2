# Drishti — one-page user guide

Drishti keeps every SQAS client, certification and lead in one place, tells you which renewals are due, and prepares follow-up emails that **you** review and send from **your own Outlook**.

## Signing in
- Open Drishti and sign in with the email and password from your invitation, or choose **Sign in with an email link**.
- **Owners** (Pramila, Mahesh) also use an authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator). The first time, scan the QR code; after that, enter the 6-digit code.
- Your profile (top right) → **My Profile & Outlook** → **Connect My Outlook** once, so emails can go from your mailbox.

## Every morning: Today
- **Today** lists the drafts Drishti prepared overnight: renewal reminders and follow-ups for leads that have gone quiet.
- Open a draft → **Edit** (or **Improve with Drishti**) → **Send**. It goes from the engagement owner's mailbox, in the same email thread.
- **Skip** a draft you don't need, or **Reschedule** it.
- Nothing is ever sent without someone pressing Send.

## The screens
| Screen | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | See pipeline value, renewals due, leads by source and quiet leads. Click any number to see the list. |
| **Renewal Radar** | See every certified client's next audit or renewal: Overdue, due in 30 days, 31–90 days, later. "Estimated" means a date was worked out from the certification date — please check it. |
| **Sales Pipeline** | Drag a lead between Lead, Proposal and On Hold. Moving it to In Progress marks it won and moves it to Delivery. |
| **Delivery** | Track won work: checklist, gap analysis, documents to collect. |
| **Clients** | Search and filter (tags, certification, status, owner, city, source, priority, contact status). Tick clients → **Email** them (one or many) or **Add tag**. Open a client for all its engagements, notes and emails. **Export to Excel** gives the 30-column sheet. |
| **WhatsApp & Calls** | Log calls. WhatsApp messages are saved as "queued" until a WhatsApp Business account is connected. |
| **Invoicing** | Record invoices; marking one paid updates Payment Status. |
| **Web Lead Form** | Add an enquiry from the website; it appears in the Pipeline without an owner until an Owner assigns one. |
| **Excel Import** (Owners) | Import the 30-column sheet (or CSV, with a column-matching step). Check the flag report before you confirm. Nothing is guessed. |
| **Settings** (Owners) | Team (invite, role, values access, deactivate), Outlook / Microsoft 365, follow-up timing, renewal cycles, retention, activity log. |

## Ask Drishti
Click **Drishti** (bottom right): "Which renewals are overdue?", "What's happening with BrightPath?", "Move Sahyadri to On Hold". Drishti only **proposes** changes; you click **Apply** (or **Dismiss**). Applied changes are logged "via Drishti".

## Good to know
- Dates show as 12-Mar-2024, times in IST, money as ₹1,00,000.
- Nothing is deleted: archive instead; an Owner can restore it.
- **Do Not Contact** on a client stops all emails and drafts for them.
- Marketing sees leads only and never sees contract values.
- Problems? Tell Freeda, with the screen name and what you clicked.
